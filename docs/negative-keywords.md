# PhotoOS — Master Negative Keywords Strategy (Google Ads & SEO)

This document outlines the **Negative Keyword Lists** for PhotoOS marketing campaigns. Using negative keywords prevents irrelevant clicks, saves ad budget, and ensures only **photography studio owners and professional photographers** visit `photoos.in`.

---

## 🎯 Negative Keyword Categories

### 1. Free / Piracy (Zero Buying Intent)
> **Goal**: Stop users looking for illegal or free cracked versions.
- `crack`
- `cracked`
- `mod apk`
- `apk download`
- `pirated`
- `torrent`
- `free download`
- `keygen`
- `hack`
- `open source`
- `github`

### 2. Photo Editing Software (Wrong Product Intent)
> **Goal**: PhotoOS is a studio management and gallery platform, NOT a raster photo editor.
- `photoshop`
- `lightroom`
- `canva`
- `snapseed`
- `picsart`
- `photo editor app`
- `background remover`
- `photo enhancer`
- `passport photo maker`
- `filters`

### 3. Face Recognition (Non-Photography & Security)
> **Goal**: Exclude biometric attendance, security systems, and AI developer searches.
- `attendance`
- `CCTV`
- `security system`
- `door lock`
- `face unlock`
- `face swap`
- `deepfake`
- `face app`
- `age filter`
- `lookalike`
- `face detection python`
- `opencv`
- `API tutorial`

### 4. B2C End Clients / Consumers
> **Goal**: PhotoOS is B2B software for photographers. We do NOT provide local wedding photography services directly to consumers.
- `"photographer near me"`
- `"wedding photographer price"`
- `"wedding photography packages"`
- `"pre wedding shoot"`
- `"photo studio near me"`
- `"book photographer"`
- `"baby shoot"`
- `"album design"`
- `"photo frame"`
- `"photo printing"`
- `"passport photo"`

### 5. Camera Hardware & Learning / Tutorials
> **Goal**: Exclude hobbyists researching cameras, lenses, or basic photography tips.
- `camera`
- `DSLR`
- `mirrorless`
- `lens`
- `tripod`
- `photography course`
- `photography classes`
- `learn photography`
- `photography tips`
- `wedding poses`
- `wedding captions`

### 6. Stock Photos & Wallpapers
> **Goal**: Exclude people searching for generic royalty-free images.
- `stock photos`
- `free images`
- `wallpaper`
- `HD images`
- `royalty free`
- `shutterstock`
- `unsplash`

### 7. Generic Consumer Cloud Storage & Social
> **Goal**: Prevent people looking for free personal cloud drives.
- `google photos`
- `google drive`
- `icloud`
- `dropbox`
- `instagram`
- `whatsapp status`
- `facebook`
- `photo storage free`

### 8. Competitor Support / Login Queries
> **Goal**: Avoid paying for existing Kwikpic/FotoOwl customers searching for account support or login.
- `"Kwikpic login"`
- `"FotoOwl login"`
- `"Kwikpic customer care"`
- `"FotoOwl support"`
- `"Kwikpic download"`
- `delete account`
- `refund`

---

## 🚀 How to Apply in Google Ads
1. Open **Google Ads** > **Tools and Settings** > **Shared Library** > **Negative Keyword Lists**.
2. Click **+** (Create List), name it `PhotoOS Master Negative List`.
3. Copy-paste the content from `public/negative-keywords.txt`.
4. Apply the list to all **Search** and **Performance Max** campaigns.
